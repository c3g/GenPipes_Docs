DRAC Account
=============

External users and genomics researchers can `apply for access <https://ccdb.alliancecan.ca>`_ to the 
`Digital Research Alliance of Canada (DRAC) <https://alliancecan.ca/en>`_, compute resources.

GenPipes on DRAC servers
-------------------------

Once you have the DRAC account, you can log in and access GenPipes pre-deployed on any of the
DRAC servers: |key_ccdb_server_name|\, |other_ccdb_server_names|\.

For details see :ref:`how to obtain a DRAC account and get started with GenPipes<docs_access_gp_pre_installed>`.
